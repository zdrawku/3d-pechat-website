## How a custom 3D printing order works

In the second half of the video we show the whole process. It's the same whether you need a single part or a small batch.

1. **Send us your idea.** A model, a sketch or a description, through the [contact form](/en/contact/), Viber or Instagram. Files go by email or Viber, as the form doesn't take attachments.
2. **Quote.** We agree the material, colour and lead time, and you get a clear price. Guide prices are on our [prices page](/en/prices/).
3. **Printing.** We make the part with quality materials and check it before it leaves.
4. **Delivery.** You receive the finished product.

No 3D model? Not a problem: we help you get from the idea to a finished model.

## When custom 3D printing is a good choice

- You need **one part or a small batch**, not thousands of pieces. 3D printing doesn't need moulds.
- The shape is **complex**. Building layer by layer makes geometry possible that other methods struggle with.
- You want a **prototype** to test before you order more.
- You're looking for a **personalised gift** with a name, a date or your own design.

The material matters too. If you're choosing between PLA, PLA+ and PLA Pro, see [the differences and how to choose](/en/blog/pla-vs-pla-plus-vs-pla-pro-vs-pln/). For more project examples, read our article on [3D printing in Sofia for your project](/en/blog/3d-printing-in-sofia-local-service/).
